Course Curriculum

Step-by-step engineering-focused drone development training

This course is offered in two DGCA-approved categories.

The training structure, curriculum, and assessments are the same for both categories. Certification is issued based on the selected drone weight class.

Small Category

Drone weight above 2 kg up to 25 kg

Medium Category

Drone weight above 25 kg up to 150 kg

Day 1: Registration & Theory

  • Registration & onboarding (15 minutes)
  • Stakeholders & applicable drone laws (1.5 hours)
  • Basic flight principles (1 hour)
  • ATC procedures & radio telephony (1 hour)
  • Fixed-wing aerodynamics & operations (1.5 hours)
  • Rotorcraft aerodynamics & operations (1.5 hours)
  • Hybrid UAV operations & aerodynamics (30 minutes)

Day 2: Theory Sessions

  • Question & answer session (30 minutes)
  • Weather and meteorology (1.15 hours)
  • Drone equipment maintenance (1.5 hours)
  • Risk assessment & analysis (1.5 hours)
  • Payload installation & utilization (1.15 hours)
  • Introduction to drone data & analysis (1.5 hours)
  • Flying & simulator training schedule briefing

Day 3: Ground & Simulator Training

  • Theory test (40 minutes)
  • Simulator training session (3 hours)
  • Pre-flight checks, takeoff, hover, cruise & landing
  • Climb, descent & turning maneuvers
  • Disorientation handling & recovery
  • Circuit flying (square, circle, orbit, figure-8)
  • Gimbal control & night flying
  • Abnormal & emergency procedures
  • Simulator evaluation test
  • Basic drone maintenance & assembling (2 hours)
  • Ground control station training
  • Flight logging & documentation

Days 4 & 5: Practical Drone Flying Training

  • Practical drone flying session (4.5 hours)
  • Introduction to Digital Sky platform
  • RPAS familiarization & safety briefing
  • Basic flight: takeoff, climb, descend & altitude hold
  • Pitch, roll & yaw control mastery
  • Disorientation handling & recovery
  • Level turns & square circuit flying
  • Figure-8 and circular flight patterns
  • Mission planning & instrument flying
  • Auto mission execution
  • Night flying operations
  • Abnormal & emergency procedures
  • Final DGCA practical test – Rotorcraft
